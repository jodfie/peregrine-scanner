Raven Scanner Return Policy

Cody
  • May 15, 2020 20:03
  • Updated

Raven Scanners can be returned within 30 days with proof of purchase (original or digital receipt) in its original packaging. 

If you would like to complete a return or have questions regarding a return, please call Customer Support at 1-800-713-9009 or support@raven.com.
